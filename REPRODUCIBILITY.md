# Reproducibility

This document describes the reproducibility scope for manuscript v1.5, **Paradoxical NMDA response amplification in a robust receptor-state regime**.

## 1. Reporting reproduction

The manuscript package contains frozen source tables plus scripts that regenerate display tables and selected manuscript figures without refitting kinetic or Bayesian models.

```bash
python -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate
pip install numpy pandas scipy matplotlib scikit-learn
cd manuscript
python code/check_reported_values.py
./code/rebuild_reporting.sh
```

The reporting scripts preserve full precision in source tables and round only publication displays.

## 2. Experimental result hierarchy

The final -40 mV classification is taken from Step15 sweep-separation output, not the earlier ±2 pA plateau-neutral rule. The final result is 5 potentiating, 5 suppressing and 1 unresolved series. Each series contains three Ro25 sweeps and three same-day same-voltage reference sweeps. Sweeps are technical repeats.

For voltage robustness, only -80, -70, -60, -40 and -20 mV are used in the current manuscript. Among the ten series resolved at -40 mV, 43 of 50 within-series negative-voltage comparisons show complete separation in the same direction, seven are unresolved, and none reverse direction.

Historical plateau-freeze files remain in the repository for provenance but are not the final classifier.

## 3. Historical Step10 parameter generation and calibration

`code/historical_step10/10_matched_parameter_rerouting.py` contains the original Step10 proposal and control-calibration implementation.

Parameter proposals were generated by Latin-hypercube sampling. The source explicitly contains the reference centers, broad bounds and log/linear sampling flags. Control calibration ranks candidates using early/primary, late/primary and charge/primary control-shape ratios. Ro25 outcomes, responder classes and the later threshold-free experimental classification are not calibration targets.

The associated frozen control targets are deposited under `data/derived/final/historical_control_calibration/experimental_targets.json`.

The current glutamate variable is a dimensionless normalized drive. It is not directly convertible to mM without an extra concentration scale that is absent from the model.

## 4. Steps 18-23

The current manuscript's model-geometry and Bayesian conclusions are based on the following analysis chain.

### Step18 — global prior geometry

Inputs: pre-calibration broad candidate table and retained Step10 broad candidates. Outputs include forcing-node screens, full-grid strong-response confirmation, parameter-geometry diagnostics and reweighting feasibility.

### Step19 — calibration/Braess frontier

Quantifies where strong responses occur along the exact historical calibration ranking and characterizes the neighborhood of witness sample 37318.

### Step20 — exact local witness compatibility

Reconstructs and validates the historical calibration score and replays historical control dynamics. It then perturbs the witness locally. The exact score reconstruction and control replay are hard gates; downstream local conclusions are not used if they fail.

### Step21 — expanded basin geometry

Samples wider log-rate boxes around the witness, estimates distances to basin boundaries along random rays, and performs a multi-start search for additional strong/control-compatible regions.

### Step22 — Bayesian occupancy

Estimates allocation to a model-defined strong-compatible component and compares restricted empirical component families. It does not identify a unique 12-dimensional biochemical prior.

### Step23 — publication consolidation

Replays threshold-sensitive cases, consolidates global versus local results, checks ordinary potentiating magnitude support and freezes publication reporting guardrails.

The exact scripts and the compact source tables needed to audit their reported outputs are deposited under `code/step_18` ... `code/step_23` and `data/derived/final/step18` ... `step23`.

## 5. Current headline numerical checks

The final repository should reproduce the following manuscript-level checks:

- -40 mV: 5 potentiating / 5 suppressing / 1 unresolved series.
- Experimental potentiating ratios: 1.636, 1.695, 1.877, 1.891 and 4.073.
- Step18 frozen strong threshold: 1.6359295; 1261/50,000 pre-calibration broad candidates are strong somewhere on the 80-node forcing grid; 1/5,000 retained broad candidates is strong somewhere.
- Step20 exact historical calibration replay: zero retained/rejected classification mismatches.
- Step20 local clouds: strong+compatible fractions 100%, 100%, 99.5% and 86.4% for ±2%, ±5%, ±10% and ±20% perturbations, respectively.
- Step21 expanded log-box joint fractions at fixed witness forcing: 93.3%, 68.2%, 31.8%, 11.5% and 2.33% for factors 1.25, 1.5, 2, 3 and 5.
- Step22 primary mean strong-state allocation approximately 0.46, with wide uncertainty; internal basin width/shape remains weakly identified.
- Step23 threshold sensitivity preserves the global/local contrast for strong thresholds 1.5-1.8.

These fractions describe model-space accessibility under specified sampling measures. They are not direct estimates of biological prevalence.

## 6. Figure 1 provenance

The representative Figure 1 traces are ABF-derived displays for the 12 October 2022 cell 1 series and its same-day reference at -40 mV. Three individually baseline-subtracted sweeps were averaged for display. The simplified display curves were recovered from an archived vector panel; numerical plateau levels come from the independent full-resolution sweep analysis, not from digitizing the plotted curve.

## 7. Acute memantine

The acute ancillary slice experiment used 30 µM memantine added to the bath. This is distinct from chronic oral memantine experiments in the cited SCA1 study and from the Ro25 analysis that forms the primary experimental result here.

## 8. Raw-data limitation

Raw ABFs are not redistributed in this repository. Therefore a clean clone can reproduce reporting and the deposited final computational analyses, but cannot independently repeat raw waveform extraction or raw-recording quality control. Any future archive of the raw ABFs should be linked as a separate data deposit rather than silently implied by the present repository.

## 9. Software environment

Core final-analysis dependencies are Python 3, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib and scikit-learn. Raw ABF extraction additionally requires pyABF. The manuscript PDFs require a standard LaTeX installation with pdfLaTeX and BibTeX.
